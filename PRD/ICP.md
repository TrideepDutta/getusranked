# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) — GetUsRanked

*Internal use only. This profile guides outreach and sales targeting — it intentionally does not appear on the public website, which stays industry-neutral.*

## 1. Primary ICP: Garment & Textile Manufacturers / Exporters

### Firmographics
- **Industry:** Garment manufacturing, textile production, apparel export.
- **Business type:** Factories/manufacturers selling B2B to brands, wholesalers, or international buyers — not primarily direct-to-consumer.
- **Company size:** Small to mid-sized manufacturers, typically owner-operated or family-run, with an export or sales function but no dedicated marketing team.
- **Geography:** India-based manufacturers targeting domestic and international buyers, though the model generalizes to other manufacturing hubs.
- **Current marketing channels:** Trade directories (IndiaMART, Alibaba), trade fairs, word-of-mouth, agent/broker relationships. Minimal to no organic search presence today.

### Buyer Persona
- **Role:** Owner, export manager, or business development lead.
- Digitally aware but not SEO-literate — understands the business need ("get more buyer inquiries") but not the mechanism behind it.
- Time-constrained, and often skeptical of agencies after a past vague "digital marketing" pitch that didn't deliver.

### Pain Points
- Relies heavily on directories and trade fairs; buyer inquiries are inconsistent and often low-quality.
- Competing manufacturers with a stronger online presence are winning inquiries they should be getting.
- Has a website (or none at all) that doesn't rank for the terms real buyers actually search.

### Buying Triggers
- A slow season or inconsistent inquiry flow.
- Noticing a competitor visibly outranking them for a shared product term.
- A referral from another manufacturer or brand who's had success with SEO or digital marketing.

### Objections & How to Address Them
- **"We don't have time for this."** → Position SEO as a background process with clear monthly reporting, not another task on their plate.
- **"We tried digital marketing before and it didn't work."** → Be specific and transparent about the process (see the Process section on the website), and offer a free audit before any commitment.
- **"Our buyers don't use Google."** → Reframe: even referral-driven buyers increasingly verify suppliers via search before reaching out.

### Where to Find Them
- Existing personal network — manufacturer and DTF print contacts from your friend's clothing brand.
- LinkedIn — export managers and manufacturing business owners.
- Industry-specific WhatsApp/Telegram groups and trade associations.
- Trade fairs and B2B directories — as a source of contact info, not a channel to advertise on.

### Messaging Angle
Lead with specific, credible knowledge of the industry — MOQ, GSM, private label, export documentation — to differentiate from generic agencies pitching a templated service they clearly don't understand.

## 2. Secondary ICP: DTF & Custom Print Suppliers
- Smaller businesses, faster sales cycle — good source for a quick first case study.
- Strong local/regional search intent ("DTF printing near me," "custom print supplier [city]").
- Same network access, via your friend's ecommerce brand's print vendors.

## 3. Who Is NOT ICP (Disqualifiers)
- Large enterprise manufacturers with in-house marketing teams and existing agency relationships.
- Businesses expecting overnight ranking results, or unwilling to commit to a 3+ month minimum engagement.
- Businesses without a functioning website or willingness to invest in one — SEO needs a foundation to work with.

## 4. How This Interacts With the Public Website
The website (getusranked.com) stays industry-neutral so it doesn't turn away other potential clients. Outreach, cold messaging, and early case-study targeting should focus on this ICP — your genuine domain knowledge and network are the real competitive edge here, even though the site itself doesn't say so explicitly.

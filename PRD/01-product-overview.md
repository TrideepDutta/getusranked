# Product Overview — GetUsRanked

## 1. Summary
GetUsRanked is an SEO agency, and this document covers both its own marketing website (getusranked.com) and the roadmap for tools beyond it. The public brand is intentionally industry-neutral, even though early client acquisition leans on a specific niche edge — see `ICP.md` for that targeting strategy.

## 2. Problem Statement
- **Business problem:** The founder is starting an SEO agency with no formal case studies or client testimonials to show prospects.
- **Market problem:** Most small and mid-sized businesses either ignore SEO entirely or get sold vague promises with no visible process behind them.

## 3. Goals
- Launch a credible, professional agency website that functions as Case Study #1 — its own ranking growth, tracked and shown publicly.
- Win the first 2–3 paying clients using a targeted outreach strategy (see `ICP.md`), even while the public site stays general-audience.
- Lay groundwork for a client-facing platform once the agency has recurring clients and a reason to build one.

## 4. Product Vision

**Phase 1 — Now:** A static, responsive marketing site — home, services, process, packages, contact — that builds trust without fabricated proof.

**Phase 2 — Next:** Lightweight client-facing tools: a real form backend, an automated free-audit generator, and a blog for organic content marketing.

**Phase 3 — Later:** A full client dashboard/portal — live rank tracking, monthly reporting, invoicing, and communication, tied into a lightweight CRM.

## 5. Target Audience
- **Public-facing site:** general small-to-mid businesses considering SEO — local/service, ecommerce, and B2B/export.
- **Actual outreach focus (private):** garment and textile manufacturers, exporters, and DTF/custom print suppliers. See `ICP.md` for full detail — this is not stated anywhere on the public site.

## 6. Success Metrics
- Organic ranking growth of getusranked.com itself, tracked monthly (the site's own case study).
- Number of qualified audit requests submitted through the contact form.
- Signed clients within the first defined outreach window.
- Once Phase 2/3 ship: audit tool usage, client dashboard adoption.

## 7. Scope

**In scope (Phase 1):** Marketing website, services/process/packages presentation, lead capture via contact form.

**Out of scope (Phase 1):** Client login, payment processing, automated reporting, blog/CMS.

## 8. Assumptions & Constraints
- No existing client testimonials or case studies at launch — addressed through transparency, the site's own growth as proof, and free audits to lower first-client risk.
- Solo or small team running the agency initially — favors a static site over a complex platform for now.
- Domain: getusranked.com.

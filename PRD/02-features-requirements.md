# Features & Requirements — GetUsRanked

## Phase 1 — Website (Now)

### 1. Navigation
Sticky header with logo, in-page links (Services, Process, Packages, About, Contact), and a primary CTA button ("Get a Free Audit"). Collapses to a labeled, accessible toggle menu on mobile.

### 2. Hero Section
Core value proposition headline and subheadline, a primary CTA (scrolls to Contact) and secondary CTA (scrolls to Process), plus a short trust/spec strip covering approach, reporting cadence, and location.

### 3. Who We Help
Three generic client categories — Local & Service Businesses, Ecommerce Brands, B2B & Export Businesses — each with a one-line description of the value delivered to that type of business.

### 4. Services
Six core SEO services presented as cards: Technical Audits, On-Page & Content Optimization, Local SEO/Google Business Profile, Link Building & Digital PR, Keyword & Competitor Research, Analytics & Reporting.

### 5. Credibility Section
Directly and honestly addresses having no testimonials yet. Shows the site's own Search Console growth as a self-updating case study, plus trust badges (Free Initial Audit, Plain-Language Reporting, No Lock-In Contracts).

### 6. Process
A four-step numbered process (Audit → Strategy → Execution → Reporting), each with a short label and description of what actually happens at that stage.

### 7. Packages
Three tiers (Bronze/Foundation, Silver/Growth, Gold/Authority), each listing who it's best for, what's included, reporting cadence, and minimum term. The middle tier is visually flagged as the recommended starting point.

### 8. Contact / Lead Capture
Form fields: Name, Company, Website/GBP link, What the business does, Email. Submission opens a pre-filled email to the agency inbox — no backend required in Phase 1.

### 9. Footer
Contact email, quick links, copyright.

---

## Phase 2 — Near-Term Additions (Next)
- Replace the mailto form with a real form backend (hosted form service or a lightweight server) that stores submissions and sends an automatic confirmation.
- Add a "Free Audit" auto-generator: pulls basic public SEO signals for a submitted URL (page speed, meta tags, indexing status) into a short automated report.
- Add a blog/content section, both for SEO content marketing and to build the agency's own organic authority.

## Phase 3 — Platform Roadmap (Later)
- Client login/dashboard: view live keyword rankings, monthly reports, and past communications in one place.
- Reporting automation: pull data from Google Search Console/Analytics APIs directly into client-facing dashboards.
- Billing/invoicing integration.
- A lightweight internal CRM to track leads, proposals, and client status.

## Introduction

Search engine optimization is no longer just about keyword density on traditional Google SERPs. Today, answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude synthesize answers directly from structured web pages.

### Key Pillars for 2026:
1. **Schema.org Structured Data**: Enable AI models to parse your entity graph cleanly.
2. **Core Web Vitals & Speed**: Speed remains a primary ranking signal for mobile indexing.
3. **High-Intent Content**: Focus on answering specific user questions with depth and clarity.

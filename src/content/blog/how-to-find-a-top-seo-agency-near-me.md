## 2. Red Flags vs. Green Flags when Searching "SEO Agency Near Me"

Use this scorecard when interviewing prospective agency partners:

* **Green Flags (High-Growth Partnership):** Custom site audits, in-house developers, revenue KPI focus
* **Red Flags (Wasted Budget & Penalties):** Instant rank guarantees, cheap monthly retainers, secret magic algorithms

### 🚩 Red Flags to Watch Out For

1. **"Instant #1 Rankings in 30 Days"**
   * Search algorithms use hundreds of factors. Any agency promising guaranteed top spots is likely using risky spam techniques that invite Google algorithmic manual actions.
2. **Generic Off-The-Shelf Monthly Packages**
   * If an agency offers fixed $299/mo packages without examining your site's technical health, domain authority, or competitive gaps, they are using automated spam software.
3. **Inability to Edit Website Code Directly**
   * If an agency requires you to hire a third-party developer to implement basic meta tags, schema markup, or site speed fixes, projects will stall indefinitely.
4. **Opaque Backlink Sourcing**
   * Refusing to disclose where backlinks originate usually indicates private blog networks (PBNs) or paid link farms that hurt your site long-term.

---

### 🟢 Green Flags of a Top SEO Agency

1. **Custom Technical Code & Site Audits**
   * The agency conducts a thorough crawl using tools like Screaming Frog and Lighthouse, identifying core web vitals errors, canonical issues, and JavaScript indexing bottlenecks.
2. **Focus on Organic Conversion & Revenue**
   * They speak in terms of leads, sales pipeline, e-commerce revenue, and cost per acquisition (CPA)—not just superficial rank metrics.
3. **Structured Data & AI Search Optimization (GEO)**
   * They demonstrate how they implement JSON-LD Schema.org graphs so your business appears in AI answers like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews.
4. **Transparent Communication & Direct Access**
   * You meet directly with senior search strategists and web developers, receiving clear monthly reporting dashboards.

## 3. Step-by-Step Vetting Framework for Choosing an SEO Agency

When searching for an **SEO agency near me**, follow this structured evaluation process:

1. **Step 1:** Initial Google & Maps Search
2. **Step 2:** Technical Audit Request
3. **Step 3:** Executive Sales Workshop
4. **Step 4:** Case Study & Reference Check
5. **Step 5:** Proposal & Contract Alignment

### Step 1: Evaluate Their Own Local Search Rankings
Look at how the agency ranks in their local market. Do they appear in the Google Map Pack and top organic positions for terms like "SEO agency near me" or "SEO agency [City]"? If an agency cannot rank their own business locally, they cannot rank yours.

### Step 2: Request a Pre-Pitch Audit
Ask prospective agencies to review your domain and provide 3 actionable technical or structural recommendations during your initial call. Top agencies will happily showcase their analytical capability.

### Step 3: Inquire About Development Stack Familiarity
Whether your site is built on WordPress, Shopify, Next.js, or custom HTML/CSS, ensure the **SEO agency** has direct hands-on experience optimizing code on your specific framework.
